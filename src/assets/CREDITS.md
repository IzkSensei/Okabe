# Sample imagery

The demonstration photographs are historic photochrom prints, reproduced from the Library of Congress Prints and Photographs Division. The catalog lists no known restrictions on reproduction. Local WebP reproductions are included so the gallery also works offline. Titles in the gallery are descriptive captions.
